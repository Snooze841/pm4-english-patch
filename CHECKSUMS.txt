WHAT IS NOT IN THIS LIST, AND WHY
---------------------------------

The zip's own hash is not inside the zip, because adding it would
change the zip and so change its hash. That is why this file sits
next to the download instead of in it, and why this file has no hash
of itself either.

Three other files in this repository are deliberately unlisted:
CHECKSUMS.txt (this file), .gitattributes and .gitignore. They are
git's own configuration, not part of the download -- nothing installs
them and nothing reads them but git. Everything that reaches your
Princess Maker 4 folder is listed above.


THE ZIP IS REPRODUCIBLE
-----------------------

Every entry in it is stored with a fixed timestamp rather than the
clock of the machine that built it, so building it twice from the
same files gives the same SHA-256. The .exe is not reproducible --
PyInstaller embeds paths and timestamps -- and SECURITY.txt says so.
